# Reading Guide

This is just a quick overview of the formatting I'm using throughout this site. While the **Player Guide** section is largely safe to read, the **Compendium** is written for both player and DM use and therefore has all sorts of spoilers thrown in. Where possible, I've used the formatting tools below to keep things easier to use, depending on what you should and shouldn't know just yet.

As a sidebar, while I'm making an effort to keep secrets secret, this setting is still in development. Things that aren't secret now might become so later, ultimately it's up to players to manage their character knowledge appropriately.

=== "Player Knowledge"

    When there's a piece of information that needs to be communicated differently to players and to DMs, I'll be making use of this tabbed content format. This should allow me to provide a reasonable level of information to you as a player.
