# Backgrounds

Think of Backgrounds as passive character abilities. They tend to provide adjustments to your core stats, and sometimes provide access to non-standard skills.

## Picking a Background

Think about the kind of character you're interested in playing. Where did they spend their time before this adventure began? What social ties do they have? Which have they left behind? Keep these questions in mind while browsing the [DdB::Backgrounds]() listings. 

Characters may have up to two backgrounds, unless one of the chosen backgrounds specifies otherwise. Most backgrounds aren't mutually exclusive, so long as you can construct a narrative reason for why your character would have both. A few backgrounds don't count against the limit, and others count twice.

## Inherited Skills

Beyond just providing narrative flavor to your character, most backgrounds will also come with a few [special skills](../skills/special/index.md) that your character will start with, often at higher than level 1.

## World Ties

Some background will also ask you to make choices about how your character has interacted with the world so far, or where. These types of connections help tie your custom character into the existing world of Dusklight.
