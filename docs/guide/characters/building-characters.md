# Building a Character

!!! info
    This guide assumes you've read through the basics of the character system. If you haven't, go back and start at [Roles at the Table](../systems/players-keepers.md)

Building a Dusklight character is a three part process, and you've got a lot of options along the way. Making use of [Archetypes](./archetypes.md) as a starting point is strongly recommended for new players, but you are also welcome to freely build your character step by step. 

This page goes over the basics of the character building process. I'd strongly recommend doing this with your Keeper to help guide your character decisions, especially if this is your first time playing.

## Step 1: Background

The only place to begin is, of course, in the beginning. So the first step in creating your character is to craft your background. All Dusklight characters can have up to two Backgrounds, with a few exceptions. You've got two options for this step. 

The easiest option is to look through the [DDB::Archetypes]() index and pick an archetype that fits the character you're interested in playing to start from. If you'd rather build something a bit further from the beaten path, you can also look through the [DDB::Backgrounds]() index directly and pick two that fit your needs.

!!! tip
    If you're picking your own backgrounds, make sure to check neither has a modifier to your **background limit**. By default, characters may only have two backgrounds. However, some backgrounds count twice, or don't count at all towards the limit.

Write down the backgrounds you pick, and make a note of the skills that come with them as well. If any of your backgrounds require you to answer questions about your character's ties to the world, do that now as well. Use the **World Setting** guide to inform these choices.

## Step 2: Starting Skills

In addition to the basic skills and your background skills, you may also select up to 2 unique skills that your character has specifically invested time into. Both of these skills start at level 1. Look through the [DDB:Skills]() archive to find skills that match your character build, and work with your Keeper to finalize these choices.

## Step 3: Guiding Questions

Finally, answer a the following questions for your character to establish who they are coming into the campaign and their window to the wider world. Your character doesn't need to know the answers to all of these questions, but it's important you think about them as you begin playing them in the world.

1. What made you want, or need, to leave your home?
2. What grand goal have you set your sights on?
3. Who might oppose you in your journey?
4. What will you do if you succeed?
