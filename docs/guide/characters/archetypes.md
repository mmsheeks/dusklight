# Archetypes

Character Archetypes are pre-built starting points for Dusklight characters that bundle together backgrounds and starting skills into collections you'll be familiar with from other systems. You can think of these as "classes" if you like, though they don't restrict your choices in character building going forward in the same way.

!!! tip
    Archetypes are the recommended way for new players to build their first character!

## Familiar Faces

To give you an idea of what these look like, let's examine some concepts you'll probably be familiar with.

### The Magic Initiate

=== "Build Summary"

    This Archetype bundles together the **Arcane Prodigy** background with the **Arcane Alumnus** background to create what one might consider the a beginner wizard adventurer. 
    
    After applying both of the backgrounds, and the modifiers from our [basic](../skills/basic.md) skills, this character would start out with these basic stats.

    !!! note "Contextual Bonuses"
        (+n\*) notation is used to indicate a bonus to a stat that is applied contextually by a skill.

    | Strength   | 2        | Charm      | 1        |
    |------------|----------|------------|----------|
    | Smarts     | 1 (+2\*) | Dexterity  | 1        |
    | Magic      | 3 (+2\*) | Max Health | 12       |
    
    They start with an enhanced ability to investigate the arcane nature of the world around them, harness the natural magic of the world to cause basic effects, and can craft basic magical tools. The player might choose to pursue a specialization in combat or healing magic next, or take another path entirely.
=== "Background: Arcane Prodigy"

    Ever since you were born, there's been something about the world around you that you can see that others seem to miss. A shimmer here, a twinkle there. As you got older, you found you could interact with that unseen force, if you tried hard enough. Now you just need to find someone to teach you how.
     
    **Magic Adept Skill: Level 3**

    > The Magic Adept skill gives you a +1 to your base magic stat per level of skill.

    **Arcane Attunement Skill: Level 2**

    > The Arcane Attunement skill gives you a +1 (per level) to **smarts** checks that relate to the arcane nature of the world.
    > 
    > At level 5, this skill grants the bonus **Arcane Influence** skill, which allows you to impart your will on the arcane elements around you directly.

=== "Background: Arcane Alumnus"

    After showing some rudimentary ability, you got yourself enrolled in one of the worlds basic schools of magic. While not the most common path, many citizens study the use of arcane tools and craft to make a living throughout the realm. Rarer still are those who study the arcane with true intent to reach a deeper understanding. Whatever your motives, you've been trained in the basics.

    **Mage Practitioner Skill: Level 2**

    > This skill allows you to use a focus item of your choosing to pull arcane energy from the world around you to cause magical effects. The exact effect of using this skill varies by application. For more information see the player guide section on [Magic](../systems/magic.md).
    > 
    > To use this ability you must make a **magic** check, with a +1 modifier per level of this skill.
     
    **Arcane Crafts Skill: Level 1**

    > If you're going to be practicing magic, it's important to understand your materials. Through study, you've gained a basic understanding of arcane crafting materials and their uses. Given the right supplies, you can craft or repair most basic magical equipment.
    > 
    > Tools crafted using this skill are imbued with a +1 modifier to a relevant stat per level of skill. This modifier only applies when using the crafted tool.

### The Street Brawler

=== "Build Summary"

    This Archetype starts with the **Street Urchin** background then piles on the **Heavy Weight** background to make a rough and tumble fighter that's ready to take on any match, even if they might lose.

    After applying both of the backgrounds, and the modifiers from our [basic](../skills/basic.md) skills, this character would start out with these basic stats.

    | Strength   | 3 (+2\*) | Charm      | 1 (+2\*) |
    |------------|----------|------------|----------|
    | Smarts     | 1 (+2\*) | Dexterity  | 1        |
    | Magic      | 0        | Max Health | 15       |

=== "Background: Street Urchin"

    It would be an understatement to say you haven't always had a glamorous life. Through one misfortune or another you found yourself relying on your quick wits and resourcefulness, and the rare good will of others, to make ends meet. It's not all bad news though, living rough teaches you things too.

    **Tough As Nails Skill: Level 1**

    > This skill grants +1 to your **strength** stat per level. In addition, if you would fall below 0 health, you instead drop unconscious at 1hp. This ability can only trigger once every 48 hours.
    
    **Found Resources Skill: Level 2**

    > This skill grants a +1 to any **smarts** or **charm** checks made to locate and acquire resources.

=== "Background: Heavy Weight"

    You're tough, you've always been tough. Someone needed to hire some muscle, and you fit the bill. You've taken enough jobs like that by now to have both the reputation and the build to match. Most of the time you only need the reputation.

    **Hand to Hand Combat Skill: Level 2**

    > This skill gives you a +1 to **strength** checks made during melee combat.
    > 
    > At higher levels, this skill grants you the ability to make an unarmed attack once per turn without expending an action point.
    
    **Defensive Build: Level 1**

    > This skill grants +2 to your endurance.
    > 
    > At level 5, this skill allows you to expend one action point not on your turn to intercept an attack on an ally within range.

## Archetypes as Starting Points

While these Archetypes are here to provide a useful starting point for common "level 1" characters, they behave differently from the more standard character classes in one important way. **Archetypes do not naturally restrain what other skills you can develop for your character**.

That is to say, there's no penalty to a character who chose a fighter-like archetype who might want to study potion craft, or a mage who wants to learn swordplay. You're not forced to invest your points in a way that makes other aspects of the game unavailable.

While this gives you a wide range of flexibility in character design, you'll often still find yourself making choices that align with your archetype. Most of the time it's better to master a specific domain rather than be mediocre at several, as you only improve the skills you focus on using or training.
