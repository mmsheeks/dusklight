# Trading & Bartering

Characters will often find themselves in a position to make trade with various merchants or barter for goods with what they have on them. While there are specific skills that guide interactions for this, these general rules apply where a character may not have any levels in the relevant skill.

## Trading

Players may use their **charm** stat to roll generic persuasion checks against merchants to determine their disposition towards offering better prices. The ultimate outcome of such a roll is determined by the Keeper. Otherwise, items are priced as listed in the [DDB::Items]() index.

### Currencies

Various different regions of the land use a variety of different currencies. To simplify this for players, all prices are converted into common **gold**, **silver**, and **copper** denominations. 

Narratively, this may result in some price fluctuation due to exchange rates. And depending on where your money is from, it might not be accepted everywhere.

1 gold = 10 silver = 100 copper

## Bartering

Whether you just don't have or want to spend the coin, or if the shop you're at doesn't barter in currency, players may also barter with their own items and services. Whether this works out will depend on the shop, but the option is always available. Most shops will honor a trade in kind, so long as you're trustworthy.
