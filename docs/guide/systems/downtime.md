# Downtime Activities

At the Keepers discretion, but at least once per session, the party will have access to period of downtime. Downtime provides opportunity to train skills, work on projects, repair gear, heal wounds, or go shopping if you're near a town.

Each player may chose up to three actions to take during downtime. Some actions can be taken multiple times in a single downtime period, but not all.

## Training Skills

Skills that are marked as being trainable during downtime may be passively trained towards leveling by spending one of your downtime activities to train. You may only train one skill per downtime, but you may spend as many of your actions on training as you wish. 

If a skill does not specify how many downtime actions each level costs, then it is at the Keeper's discretion to choose. The Keeper must tell players how many downtime actions leveling a skill will cost if asked. Training is cumulative over multiple downtime periods.

## Personal Projects

Got something you need to craft? A memoir you're writing out? Maybe a new spell you've almost got the hang of.

Players can define their own personal projects with the help of the Keeper. Choose a goal, and the keeper will tell you how many times you'll need to work on the project to reach it as well as any other requirements to finishing the task.

These can take a while to complete, but usually come with upgrades for your character in the form of new gear or skills.

## Repair Gear

!!! tip
    This is a party action! If other members of your party have broken gear and the repair materials, they may also repair their gear. Only one character needs to take this action per downtime.

If something you need broke while you were on your adventure and you've got the materials to fix it, you can spend a downtime action to repair your gear.

## Healing Wounds

You can spend one downtime action to heal your own wounds. Roll a flat **2d6** and recover that many hit points, up to your maximum. This action may only be taken once per character each downtime.

### Healing Grave Wounds

If you are [Gravely Wounded](./character-death.md#gravely-wounded), you may instead stabilize your wounds at 4hp, but clearing them will require extra conditions to be met.

In order to clear the **Gravely Wounded** condition, you must be taking downtime near a medical facility of some type, or a member of your party must have a healing ability that allows you to clear grave wounds.

If you are near a medical facility, you may spend your full downtime resting there instead of with your party to clear your wounds. Heal normally, starting from 4hp. If you are relying on a party member, the duration and efficacy of the healing will vary based on the skill they are using.


## Shopping

While it can be fun to roleplay haggling down the local merchant, sometimes you just need more rope. You can spend one downtime action to visit nearby shops (given there are any) and trade for materials at fair market prices.

This action may only be taken once per character each downtime.
