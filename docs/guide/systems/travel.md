# Traveling

Dusklight features a world map that is quite geographically large, with multiple options for travel across it. However most players will travel using the overland road network. Traveling long distances through the world isn't without risk either, as not all regions of the world are entirely peaceful or without dangers.

When traveling long distances, the party decides as a group how they would like to travel

 - **Fast, but Loud (-2)** allows you to cover the most ground, but leaves you exposed to risks you might not see coming.
 - **Moderate Pace (even)** lets you move a bit slower, but with less risk of a surprise. You still might run into some encounters.
 - **Slow, and Quiet (+2)** gives you the upper hand at the cost of speed. You won't cover much ground, but you'll see them first.

The Keeper will make a flat group **2d6** check with the modifier from your choice in the list above, which will determine what you run into on your day of travel. Keep in mind, some encounters may be unavoidable.

## Travel Pace

There are general guidelines, adjust as makes sense for your party. Some travel options don't apply at certain paces because the benefit could not be achieved.

| Pace     | On Foot    | By Mount   | By Carriage  |
|----------|------------|------------|--------------|
| Fast     | 15km       | 50km       | 40km         |
| Moderate | 8 to 12km  | 40km       | 30km         |
| Slow     | 3 to 5km   | N/A        | N/A          |
