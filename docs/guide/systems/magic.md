# Magic Systems

Dusklight has a several different magical origins and types of magic within the setting. By far the most common in the current age, and the type of magic practiced by most player characters, is Arcane magic.

Arcane magic taps into the various arcane elements present throughout the natural world to manipulate reality with willpower. This type of magic is well suited for small tasks, considered **Common Magic**. For more complex tasks, Arcane magic is limited by the nature of its power source. Accomplishing large tasks requires the use of **Big Magic**.

## Common Magic

Most common effects — things like summoning a flame, moving an object, filling a glass with water — can be accomplished through the use of common magic. This type of magic is flexible, so long as the task isn't too large, and is what most magic based skills utilize.

The main limits on common magic are the attunement, willpower, and imagination of the caster, as well as the available arcane energy in the natural world around them at the time.

Common magic is fueled by the energy of naturally occurring arcane elements, which are consumed when a caster uses them. Most casters carry some type of arcane battery with them, which allows them to cast without negatively impacting the environment around them.

## Big Magic

For complex tasks where simple Arcane magic won't suffice, players turn to **Big Magic**. Various different options may be available at any time to use big magic, depending on where you are in your story. You'll have to work with the keeper to plan the details.

Big magic often requires multiple casters, possibly a specific location, or maybe an entire ritual in order to cast. It takes planning and careful execution to get right, and if it goes wrong the results can be fatal. Depending on the skills and social connections you have access to, you may be able to find ways to pull of Big Magic tasks that have fewer risks than using Arcane magic specifically.

Character Resurrections are an example of something that is always big magic.

## Backlash

Mechanically, there is nothing to stop a caster from attempting to use magic in a way that exceeds the available energy in their battery and the surrounding natural world. When this happens, the spell will use their own internal arcane energy to supplement the casting until the effect is achieved, or the caster is knocked unconscious. This effect is called Backlash.

Backlash drains character HP in order to supplement the arcane energy necessary to cast the spell. If the character is [gravely wounded](./character-death.md#gravely-wounded) by this process, they immediately pass out and the spell effect ends.

Backlash will only ever happen on a failed roll, and the keeper should give you warning of the potential effect before you use the skill involved. That doesn't mean they have to.
