# Character Deaths

As noted in the [basic character stats](../characters/stats.md) article, most characters in the world of Dusklight are not expected to have outrageously large health pools. That doesn't mean that monsters can't deal substantial damage. No matter what level you are, some things will still just kill you. So, it's important to know how death works in the Dusklight system.

## Life is Finite

The most important thing to know about life and death in the world of Dusklight is that death is inevitable. All mortal creatures die, and there is a limit to how far you can go to avoid this. 

Health is a resource that must be replenished, it does not refill on its own. You can use various skills to heal, or spend time tending your wounds in [Downtime](./downtime.md) to recover, but you likely won't be fully healed quickly if you are seriously injured.

## Gravely Wounded

Any creature in the world of Dusklight is considered gravely wounded if it has less than one quarter of its health remaining. To simplify this for players, any player character lower than 4hp is considered gravely wounded.

Once a character enters this state, they must seek medical aid beyond what can be found in the field in order to recover. Field medics and healing magic may keep the character alive, but they won't be able to recover without being treated.

Gravely Wounded characters suffer a -1 penalty to all rolls, and are often affected by other conditions that make it hard to continue fighting.

## Death is Instant

With very few exceptions, once you're dead you're dead. A limited number of skills provide the ability to prevent a character from reaching 0hp, but once you are truly out of health points, you are dead. There are no saving throws in Dusklight.

## Revival has Costs

Characters may be brought back to the realm of the living via various means, usually requiring the use magic beyond the capacity of a single caster. However, there is no path back to life for a dead character that does not carry some heavy cost. 

Revived characters are often changed, and while they may resemble the previous living person, they should not be considered the same and may not have the same motives. This may be as simple as a change in eye color, or the loss of a body part. It may also take the form of more sinister transformations.
