# Roles at the Table

Dusklight is a collaborative storytelling game, with a focus on narrative improv and quick high-stakes combat. Everyone at the table should be working together as a team to tell the story of our party of heroes — or villains.

## Players & The Party

Most Dusklight players will run their own player character, usually as part of the larger party. Players work together to solve mysteries, overcome obstacles, defeat the bad guys, and save the day! Or at least go home richer than they were when they left.

Player Characters are the heroes or anti-heroes of the story. No matter what your role, everyone works together to make sure the story being told around them is a success.

## The Keeper

The Keeper is in charge of the campaign, the NPCs, and everything else in the world that the player characters interact with. Keepers design obstacles for the characters to overcome, and challenges that they might perish to, but always with the goal of giving the heroes their moment.

## A Note on Safety

Both the players and the Keeper are responsible for creating a safe space for everyone at the table collaboratively. The [TTRPG Safety Toolkit](https://ttrpgsafetytoolkit.com/) has a variety of different tools available for creating safer gaming spaces. The main goal is to ensure everyone participating has enthusiastically consented to the action at the table.
