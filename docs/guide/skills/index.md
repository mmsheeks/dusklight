# Character Skills

After your core stats and backgrounds, the rest of your character is built up out of your Skills. There is no limit to how many skills a character may have, but each skill will only be as valuable as the amount of time you invest into training it.

!!! tip
    This section of the guide goes over the core concepts around Skills. Next we'll cover [Archetypes](../archetypes/index.md), which provide easy to use starting points for most character builds that include a base set of skills. So don't get too worried about figuring out your build just yet.

## Simple Checks

For most common actions where a skill isn't necessary, a simple ability check against your character's [core stats](../characters/character-stats.md) is made. You roll **2d6** plus the points you have in the relevant skill. 

Normally, anything over a 6 is at least a partial success, and rolling 12 or higher counts as an enhanced success. These bounds may be modified by the Keeper, depending on the context.

## Training Skills

All skills start at level one. Most skills can be leveled up beyond that to increase their potency or unlock bonus effects. Increasing the levels of your individual skills can only be done by spending time in game using them, with special skills often requiring you to meet extra conditions.

## Basic Skills

Basic skills are available to all characters. This covers things most common adventurers would need to do like scouting an area, looking for clues, and fighting or defending.

At level one, these don't provide too much benefit. However, most basic skills can be trained during [Downtime](../systems/downtime.md), and will also increase in level as you use them for relevant tasks during the campaign.
