Hello there! Welcome to the setting and player guide for my custom setting, Dusklight. This setting and the custom ruleset that I've built to use along side it are the result of years of tinkering away with different ideas. This is actually the second re-write of the entire thing, and I believe the most refined version I've got yet. It's a work in progress, but I think you'll have fun with it.

## Time in the Setting

I wanted to include a note here on time. Throughout the compendium and guide you'll see reference to "year X". These refer to time within the current known era of history. Dusklight uses a system of **Nocturnes and Daybreaks** that creates isolated periods of time.

**Daybreaks** describe times of abundance, growth, and prosperity. These are periods of time in which societies can form, develop technologies and traditions, create art, and flourish.

**Nocturnes** are times of catastrophe, be that swift, or slow. History may or may not survive a Nocturne, leading to new eras in the Daybreaks following them without knowledge of the old.

The current historical era spans from the start of [The Second Daybreak](./compendium/events/daybreaks.md#second-daybreak), which was roughly 1200 years ago. For sake of flexibility, I'm not going to be exactly strict on dates. Expect things to be in the correct order, and roughly paced as intended, but some things may move around a bit. The important thing here is to keep in mind the world is much older than its inhabitants can recall.
